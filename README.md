# Ledgr

**A LOCAL & PRIVATE expense tracker that reads your bank statement, so you don't have to type every payment.**

No accounts, no tracking. Your data stays in your browser, on your device.

## Import your bank statement
Download the statement from your bank or UPI app (PhonePe works) and import it.

- Only use CSV or Excel files, to be used as downloaded
- Columns, dates and debit/credit are detected automatically
- Duplicates are skipped, so overlapping statements are safe
- Known merchants (Swiggy, Zomato, Amazon, Uber...) are categorized automatically
- Processed on your device, never uploaded

Settings → Import bank statement.

## Features
- Balance, income and expenses by month
- Reports with donut and bar charts
- Daily budget meter
- Custom categories with icons and colours
- Savings goals and category budgets
- Insights on where your money went
- Backup and restore (JSON, CSV)

## ⭐ Add it to your Home Screen
**Do this first.** Open the app on your phone, then use the browser menu → **Add to Home Screen**. It then works like a normal app and keeps your data safer.

## Privacy
Everything is stored in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
